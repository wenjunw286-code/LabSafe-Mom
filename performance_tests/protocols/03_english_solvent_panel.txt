Performance Test Protocol 03: English organic solvent panel

Purpose
Stress English synonym matching, CAS extraction, volatile solvent rules, and mixed hazard levels.

Reagents
Formaldehyde, CAS 50-00-0, 20 mL, fixation, open container.
Methanol, CAS 67-56-1, 150 mL, fixation and washing.
Chloroform, CAS 67-66-3, 30 mL, organic extraction.
Dichloromethane DCM, CAS 75-09-2, 30 mL, extraction.
Toluene, CAS 108-88-3, 20 mL, solvent wash.
Xylene, CAS 1330-20-7, 40 mL, clearing.
Acetonitrile, CAS 75-05-8, 100 mL, HPLC mobile phase.
Ethanol, CAS 64-17-5, 100 mL, surface cleaning.

Procedure
Prepare samples on an open bench.
Perform solvent extraction in uncapped glass tubes.
Vortex for 30 seconds and centrifuge at room temperature.
Transfer organic phase by pipette.
The fume hood is not running during part of the operation.

Exposure notes
Operator is pregnant.
Solvent work is repeated daily for one week.
No respiratory protection is used.

Expected
Overall risk should be high or critical. Volatile chlorinated and aromatic solvents should be prioritized.
