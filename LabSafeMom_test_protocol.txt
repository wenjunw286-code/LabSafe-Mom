LabSafeMom Test Protocol

Experiment title:
Immunofluorescence fixation and staining workflow for cultured HeLa cells.

Objective:
Evaluate a typical cell biology protocol that includes fixatives, organic solvents,
DNA stains, and standard exposure controls for pregnancy and lactation risk screening.

Materials and reagents:
- HeLa cells cultured on glass coverslips.
- 4% paraformaldehyde (PFA) solution for fixation, 10 mL per sample batch.
- Methanol, CAS 67-56-1, pre-chilled at -20 C, 20 mL.
- Dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO), CAS 67-68-5, used to dissolve a small-molecule inhibitor.
- DAPI nuclear stain, stock solution diluted 1:1000.
- Phosphate-buffered saline (PBS).
- Blocking buffer containing bovine serum albumin.

Procedure:
1. Transfer coverslips into a certified chemical fume hood.
2. Fix cells with 4% paraformaldehyde for 15 minutes at room temperature.
3. Remove PFA waste into a labeled hazardous chemical waste bottle.
4. Permeabilize cells with chilled methanol for 5 minutes.
5. Prepare inhibitor working solution in DMSO at low volume under the fume hood.
6. Stain nuclei with diluted DAPI for 5 minutes.
7. Wash samples three times with PBS and mount coverslips.

Exposure controls:
- All fixation and solvent handling steps are performed in a chemical fume hood.
- Operators wear a buttoned lab coat, safety goggles, and double nitrile gloves.
- PFA and methanol waste are collected separately as hazardous waste.
- Pregnant or lactating personnel should delegate PFA preparation and methanol handling when possible.

Expected test behavior:
The system should identify paraformaldehyde/PFA, methanol, DMSO, and DAPI.
The final report should not return a zero score. PFA and methanol should contribute
substantial pregnancy risk, while DMSO and DAPI should add route-specific caution.
