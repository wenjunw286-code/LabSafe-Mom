Protocol: DNA Extraction and PCR

Chemicals:
- Ethanol (CAS: 64-17-5) 70% solution
- Sodium chloride (CAS: 7647-14-5) for buffer preparation
- Tris-HCl buffer, pH 8.0  
- EDTA 0.5M solution
- Ethidium bromide (CAS: 1239-45-8) for gel staining
- Chloroform (CAS: 67-66-3) for organic extraction
- Isopropanol for DNA precipitation

Procedure:
1. Weigh 5g of tissue sample
2. Homogenize tissue in lysis buffer
3. Add 500ul chloroform, vortex vigorously, centrifuge at 12000rpm for 10 minutes
4. Transfer aqueous phase to new tube, add equal volume isopropanol
5. Wash DNA pellet with 1ml 70% ethanol, centrifuge
6. Resuspend DNA in 50ul TE buffer
7. Set up PCR reaction with Taq polymerase
8. Run agarose gel electrophoresis, stain with ethidium bromide
9. Visualize DNA bands under UV transilluminator

Safety notes: All procedures performed on open bench. Standard lab PPE used.
